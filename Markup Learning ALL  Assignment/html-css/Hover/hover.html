Sheikh Mujibur Rahman

(Also known as Sheikh Mujib;17 March 1920 – 15 August 1975) was the founder of Bangladesh. He first served as the titular president of the Provisional Government of Bangladesh between April 1971 and January 1972. He then served as Prime Minister of Bangladesh from the Awami League between January 1972 and January 1975.
